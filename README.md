# Circular Magnetic Field Star-Core

**Special experimental magnetic component**

## Overview
This is a custom / prototype magnetic assembly designed to generate a continuous **circular (toroidal-style) magnetic field**.

The five hematite elements produce local magnetic fields that slightly overlap and touch around the circumference of the ring, forming a closed circular field path. The copper star acts as an independent non-magnetic core, and the outer stainless steel ring serves as a magnetic shield.

## Physical Construction (Base Device)

| Component              | Material              | Role                                      |
|------------------------|-----------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Outer ring             | Stainless steel       | Magnetic shield / containment             |
| Central star           | Copper                | Separate non-magnetic / conductive core   |
| Five perimeter elements| Faceted hematite      | Magnetic field sources                    |
| Joints at star points  | Gold solder           | Non-magnetic (diamagnetic) fastening      |

- The copper star is fixed at its five tips to the inner edge of the stainless ring using **gold solder**.
- The five hematite stones are positioned in the inter-point spaces of the star.
- The assembly creates a circular field in which the individual fields from the hematite pieces "touch slightly" around the ring.

## Current Upgraded Configuration (Neodymium-Boosted + Saucer-Focused)

The base star-core assembly is now **locked in place** on a large **2×2×1 inch neodymium “monster” magnet** and focused by a metal saucer.

### Components in the boosted setup:
- **Base power source**: 2×2×1" neodymium magnet (very strong axial field)
- **Focusing element**: Rusty circular metal saucer that concentrates and directs the combined field upward
- **Active assembly**: Copper-star + hematite ring sitting inside the saucer, locked to the neodymium below

### Resulting behavior:
The strong vertical field from the neodymium supercharges the circular field generated by the five hematite elements. The saucer acts as a magnetic lens, focusing the combined energy into a tighter, more intense upward column.

This turns the original passive circular-field device into an **actively boosted, directionally focused magnetic concentrator**.

## Functional Description

- **Circular field generation**: The spaced hematite elements create a near-continuous circular magnetic path.
- **Copper core**: Acts as a separate core element, electrically and magnetically distinct from the outer shield.
- **Outer shield**: The stainless steel ring contains and shapes the field, reducing external leakage.
- **Gold solder**: Chosen for its diamagnetic properties so it does not interfere with the magnetic circuit.
- **Neodymium boost**: Massively amplifies field strength.
- **Saucer focus**: Concentrates and directs the field upward.

This is **not** jewelry and is **not** a standard commercial transformer core. It is a specialized experimental or research-oriented magnetic component, now operating in a high-power boosted configuration.

## Conceptual Parallel
The geometry and layered field approach share conceptual similarities with the Iron Man Arc Reactor (central core + surrounding field elements + containment ring), though this is a pure magnetic experimental device rather than a power reactor.
