# Circular Magnetic Field Star-Core

**Special experimental magnetic component**

## Overview
This is a custom / prototype magnetic assembly designed to generate a continuous **circular (toroidal-style) magnetic field**.

The five hematite elements produce local magnetic fields that slightly overlap and touch around the circumference of the ring, forming a closed circular field path. The copper star acts as an independent non-magnetic core, and the outer stainless steel ring serves as a magnetic shield.

## Physical Construction (Base Device)

| Component              | Material              | Role                                      |
|------------------------|-----------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Outer ring             | Stainless steel       | Magnetic shield / containment             |
| Central star           | Copper                | Separate non-magnetic / conductive core   |
| Five perimeter elements| Faceted hematite      | Magnetic field sources                    |
| Joints at star points  | Gold solder           | Non-magnetic (diamagnetic) fastening      |

## Current Experimental Stack (Latest Configuration)

**Bottom to top:**

1. **2×2×1 inch neodymium “monster” magnet** — primary high-strength field source
2. **Play-Doh** — protective cushion / spacer on the magnet
3. **Star-core assembly** (stainless ring + copper star + five hematite elements) seated in the focusing saucer
4. **1/8-inch thick aluminum cross** resting on top of the Play-Doh

### Roles of the new layers:
- **Play-Doh**: Soft protective buffer that prevents hard contact with the neodymium and provides slight spacing.
- **Aluminum cross (1/8")**: Highly conductive, non-magnetic. In the strong field it generates eddy currents and can act as a field shaper, damper, or heat sink depending on orientation.

## Functional Description

- Circular field generation via the five spaced hematite elements
- Copper star as independent core
- Stainless outer ring as shield
- Gold solder (diamagnetic) at the star points
- Neodymium base for massive field amplification
- Saucer for upward focusing
- Play-Doh for protection and spacing
- Aluminum cross for conductive interaction with the field

This remains a specialized experimental magnetic concentrator, not jewelry or a commercial transformer.
