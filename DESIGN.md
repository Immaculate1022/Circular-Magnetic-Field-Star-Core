# Design Specification – Circular Magnetic Field Star-Core

## Design Intent
Create a compact magnetic assembly that produces a continuous circular magnetic field using discrete magnetic elements (hematite) arranged around a central non-magnetic copper star core, contained by a stainless steel shield ring.

## Key Design Principles
1. **Circular field continuity**: Five magnetic elements spaced so their fields slightly overlap.
2. **Separated core**: Copper star is electrically and magnetically independent of the outer ring.
3. **Shielding**: Stainless outer ring contains the field.
4. **Non-interfering joints**: Gold solder (diamagnetic) at the five star points.

## Materials
- Outer ring: Stainless steel (non- or weakly magnetic depending on grade)
- Star: Copper (highly conductive, non-magnetic)
- Magnetic elements: Faceted hematite (or magnetic hematite)
- Fastening: Gold solder at star points

## Assembly Notes
- Position the copper star concentrically inside the stainless ring.
- Secure each of the five star tips to the inner circumference of the ring with gold solder.
- Place one faceted hematite element in each of the five inter-point gaps.
- Ensure the hematite pieces are held firmly (mechanical fit or additional non-magnetic retention if needed).

## Observed Behavior
- The device produces a circular magnetic field in which the individual fields from the five hematite pieces touch / slightly overlap around the ring.
- The copper center functions as a distinct core.
- The outer ring acts as a shield.

## Status
Prototype / experimental piece. No commercial part number identified.
